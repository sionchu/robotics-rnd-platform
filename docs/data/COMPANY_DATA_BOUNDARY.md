# Company Data Boundary

The personal private repository and personal workstations are not company
storage. When ownership or transfer rights are unclear, the decision is
`DO NOT SYNC`.

Never transfer or commit:

- company CAD or CAD-derived geometry;
- production robot poses, coordinates, workspaces, recipes, or calibration;
- internal hostnames, IP addresses, network diagrams, serial numbers, or credentials;
- confidential Mech-Mind, Mech-Eye, Zivid, camera, rosbag, MCAP, or point-cloud captures;
- internal screenshots, logs, UI, protocols, customer material, or process details;
- vendor SDK binaries, examples, license files, or models without redistribution rights.

Generic interfaces, independently implemented algorithms, public standards,
synthetic examples, and personally created lab data may be used only after an
ownership and provenance review. Sanitization is not permission: a source must
still be authorized for transfer.

If restricted material is found in a worktree, do not delete or redistribute it.
Keep it untracked, stop the sync path, and follow the repository incident process
in `SECURITY_AND_IP.md`.
