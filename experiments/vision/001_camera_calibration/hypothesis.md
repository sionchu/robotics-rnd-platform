# Question and Hypothesis

## Question

Can 28 diverse noisy synthetic checkerboard observations recover known
intrinsics and provide stable reprojection metrics?

## Hypothesis

With seed `20260823` and 0.15 px corner noise, relative `fx` and `fy` error will
each be below 0.5%, principal-point error below 2 px, distortion-vector L2 error
below 0.02, and mean reprojection error below 0.3 px.
